Qwen3.5-4B is a compact, natively multimodal foundation model from Alibaba Cloud, released in March 2026 as part of the Qwen3.5 small model series. It utilizes a hybrid architecture combining Gated Delta Networks (a form of linear attention) with sparse Mixture-of-Experts.

### Key Specifications
*   **Parameters:** 4 Billion
*   **Architecture:** Hybrid (Gated Delta Networks + Gated Attention)
*   **Capabilities:** Natively multimodal (text, image, and video), 201 languages supported.
*   **Context Window:** 262,144 tokens (extensible to 1M via YaRN).
*   **License:** Apache 2.0

### Benchmark Performance
While specific point-in-time scores can vary based on evaluation frameworks and post-training versions, Qwen3.5-4B has been noted for high performance relative to its size, often cited alongside other models in its class:

*   **MMLU-Pro:** Reported scores around **79.1%**.
*   **GPQA (Diamond):** Reported scores around **76.2%**.
*   **Coding & Reasoning:** The model is designed for high-throughput tasks, with DeepInfra (providing the reasoning version) demonstrating efficient latency (0.45s TTFT) and output speeds (approx. 250 tokens/sec).
*   **Comparison:** It is frequently compared against other small models (such as those in the Gemma or Phi series). Some community benchmarks and discussions (e.g., on Reddit/LocalLLaMA) highlight that while the model is highly efficient, users should be aware that benchmark results on hosting platforms may sometimes reflect full-precision performance rather than the specific quantized (e.g., 4-bit) versions often used locally.
