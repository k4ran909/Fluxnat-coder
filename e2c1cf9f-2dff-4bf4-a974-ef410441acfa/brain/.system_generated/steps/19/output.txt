Meta's **Llama 4** model family, which includes the **Scout** and **Maverick** variants, was released on **April 5, 2025**[1][2]. These models are part of an open-weights release designed to provide high-performance, natively multimodal AI capabilities[3][4][5].

### Key Details of Llama 4 Scout and Maverick

*   **Architecture:** Both models utilize a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architecture[3][6], which allows them to achieve high intelligence while maintaining computational efficiency by only activating a subset of parameters for each token processed[7][8].
*   **Llama 4 Scout:**
    *   **Scale:** 109 billion total parameters (17 billion active)[7][5][8].
    *   **Strengths:** Designed for efficiency, it can run on a single high-end GPU (such as an NVIDIA H100) using quantization[7][9][10].
    *   **Context Window:** Features an industry-leading **10 million token context window**, making it exceptionally capable of processing vast amounts of data, such as entire codebases or long documents[7][6][10].
*   **Llama 4 Maverick:**
    *   **Scale:** 400 billion total parameters (17 billion active)[7][8].
    *   **Strengths:** A larger, more versatile model with 128 experts, providing higher reasoning and coding performance comparable to leading proprietary models at the time of its release[7][11][10].
    *   **Context Window:** Supports a 1 million token context window[7][12][8].

### Availability and Licensing
*   **Open Source:** The models were released under the **Llama Community License**, allowing for both research and commercial use[3][13][14].
*   **Access:** They are available for download on **llama.com** and **Hugging Face**[1][11].
*   **Ecosystem:** The models are supported by various tools and platforms, including Ollama (for local execution)[14][15], AWS SageMaker JumpStart[16][17], and IBM watsonx.ai[18].

A third model, **Llama 4 Behemoth** (a 2 trillion parameter model), was announced alongside Scout and Maverick but remained in the training or preview stage at the time of the initial launch[1][6][11].
