For the models you mentioned, there are several "abliterated" (uncensored) versions available on Hugging Face. These models have undergone weight-editing processes to remove the internal "refusal" directions typically present in instruction-tuned LLMs.

### **Qwen 2.5 Coder 3B Abliterated**
There are multiple versions and formats of this model available, often maintained by community members:
*   **Hugging Face Search:** You can find various implementations by searching for `Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct-abliterated`[1][2][3].
*   **Formats:** Versions exist in **GGUF** (for local CPU/GPU inference via `llama.cpp`)[4][1][3], **EXL2**[2], and even **MNN** for on-device mobile deployment[5].
*   **Creator/Source:** Notable contributors include `bartowski`[4], `criscar22`[1], and `huihui-ai`[2][6].

### **Phi-4 Mini Abliterated**
Because Phi-4-mini has a specific architecture that can make standard abliteration difficult (often requiring a "layer sweep" to locate the refusal crystallization point), specialized versions have been developed:
*   **Hugging Face:** Search for `Phi-4-Mini-Abliterated`[7][8].
*   **Key Repositories:**
    *   `DuoNeural/Phi-4-Mini-Abliterated`[7]
    *   `huihui-ai/Phi-4-mini-reasoning-abliterated`[9]
    *   Various GGUF quantizations are also available from community uploaders[10][8][11].

### **Gemma 4 Abliterated**
Gemma 4 models (including the 12B and larger variants) have several abliterated/uncensored versions. Because these are multimodal models, some versions are specifically noted for preserving vision/audio capabilities alongside the text-based abliteration[12][13][14].
*   **Hugging Face:** Look for `gemma-4-*-abliterated-uncensored`[12][15].
*   **Key Repositories:**
    *   `aoiandroid/gemma-4-12B-it-abliterated-uncensored`[12]
    *   `mradermacher` provides widespread GGUF support for these models[15].
    *   `huihui-ai` also offers various Gemma 4 abliterated models[16][17].

### **Important Considerations**
*   **Safety Warning:** Abliterated models have had their safety filters significantly reduced[6][16][18]. They may produce controversial, sensitive, or inappropriate content. They are generally intended for research, testing, or controlled environments[6][19].
*   **Refusal Patterns:** Some researchers note that certain models (like Gemma 4) may exhibit "delayed refusal," where they provide a helpful introduction before refusing. Verify the documentation on the specific model card if you encounter unexpected behavior[14].
*   **Verification:** Always check the "Model Card" on the Hugging Face page to ensure the model was abliterated using a methodology you trust (e.g., orthogonalization of the refusal direction)[19].
