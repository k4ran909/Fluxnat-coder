Gemma 4 was released by Google DeepMind on **April 2, 2026**[1][2]. The entire family of Gemma 4 models is released under the **Apache 2.0 license**, making them fully open-source and permissible for commercial use[1][2][3].

### The 26B A4B Model
The **26B A4B** (often referred to as 26B-A4B) is a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** model[2][4]. Its "A4B" designation refers to its active parameter count[5]:

*   **Total Parameters:** 26 billion[6][4] (specifically 25.2 billion)[6][7].
*   **Active Parameters:** 3.8 billion (often rounded to 4B) active per token during inference[5][6][7].
*   **Architecture:** This MoE architecture allows the model to deliver performance comparable to much larger dense models while maintaining the computational efficiency of a smaller model[7].

### Gemma 4 Family Overview
The Gemma 4 family includes four primary sizes designed for different hardware constraints[2][4]:

| Model | Architecture | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **E2B** | Dense (Effective 2B) | Edge devices, smartphones[2][8] |
| **E4B** | Dense (Effective 4B) | Edge devices, laptops[2][8] |
| **26B A4B** | Mixture-of-Experts | Workstations, cost-efficient inference[2][8] |
| **31B** | Dense | Frontier intelligence/workstations[2][8] |

### Key Features
*   **Multimodal:** All models in the Gemma 4 family support native text and image input[4][8]. Larger models (26B and 31B) also support video, while edge variants (E2B and E4B) include native audio support[2][9].
*   **Context Window:** Up to 256K tokens for medium/workstation models, with 128K for edge variants[2][8].
*   **Capabilities:** Features include native function calling, structured output, and configurable "thinking" modes for advanced reasoning[10][8][11].
