The **Qwen3.5** series, released by Alibaba Cloud in February and March 2026, represents a major leap in open-weight multimodal model performance, featuring a hybrid architecture (Gated Delta Networks combined with sparse Mixture-of-Experts) and native multimodal capabilities.

### Qwen3.5 Overview & Benchmarks
The flagship **Qwen3.5-397B-A17B** is designed for high-efficiency, frontier-level performance, activating only 17B parameters per forward pass.

    *   **AIME 2026:** 91.3
*   **Small Models (0.8B, 2B, 4B, 9B):** Released in March 2026, these models are optimized for edge devices and low-compute environments.

### Qwen2.5-Coder Series
The **Qwen2.5-Coder** series (released late 2024) focuses on code generation, reasoning, and repair. It is built on the Qwen2.5 architecture and trained on over 5.5 trillion tokens of code-related data.

*   **Qwen2.5-Coder-3B:** 
    *   Designed as a lightweight, efficient coding assistant.
    *   Benchmarks show it performs strongly in code-related tasks (generation, completion, and repair) relative to its size, though it is superseded by the newer Qwen3.5 small series for general multimodal tasks.
*   **Qwen2.5-Coder-7B:** 
    *   Known for outperforming larger models like DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite and CodeStral-22B at the time of its release.
    *   It maintains competitive performance in mathematics and general language tasks alongside its specialized coding capabilities.
