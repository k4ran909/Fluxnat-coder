### About the Model
The model **`huihui-ai/Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct-abliterated`** is a fine-tuned, "abliterated" version of the official `Qwen/Qwen2.5-Coder-3B-Instruct` model[2][4]. 

*   **Abliteration:** This process typically involves modifying the model's internal weights to remove or reduce the "refusal" behaviors often present in base instruction-tuned models, aiming to make them less prone to declining requests or giving "AI assistant" style refusals[5][6].
*   **Base Architecture:** It is based on the Qwen2.5-Coder-3B series, which is a code-specific model developed by the Qwen team at Alibaba Cloud, designed for high-performance coding tasks[7][8].
*   **Usage:** You can find this model and its derivatives (such as quantized versions for specific hardware) on Hugging Face[9][10].
